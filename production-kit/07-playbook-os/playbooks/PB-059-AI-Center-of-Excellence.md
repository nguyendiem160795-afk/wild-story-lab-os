# PB-059 --- AI Center of Excellence (AI CoE)

> **Module:** 08-enterprise-governance **Playbook ID:** PB-059
> **Version:** 1.0.0 **Status:** Stable

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Summary

Define an Enterprise AI Center of Excellence (AI CoE) that establishes
standards, governance, expertise, reusable assets, and strategic
leadership for scaling AI across the organization.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

Create a centralized capability that accelerates AI adoption, promotes
best practices, governs AI initiatives, and supports business units
throughout the AI lifecycle.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Value

-   Standardize AI delivery.
-   Accelerate innovation.
-   Improve governance consistency.
-   Increase reuse of AI assets.
-   Build enterprise AI capability.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Prerequisites

-   PB-058 Organization Change Management completed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Inputs

## Required

-   Enterprise AI Strategy
-   Governance Framework
-   AI Asset Catalog
-   Skills Matrix
-   Business Roadmap

## Optional

-   Innovation Pipeline
-   Technology Roadmap
-   Budget Plans

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Outputs

-   AI CoE Charter
-   Operating Model
-   Standards Library
-   Capability Roadmap
-   KPI Dashboard

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Workflow

1.  Define AI CoE vision and mission.
2.  Establish governance structure.
3.  Assign roles and responsibilities.
4.  Build standards and reusable assets.
5.  Support AI delivery teams.
6.  Measure performance and maturity.
7.  Drive continuous improvement.
8.  Scale enterprise AI capabilities.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# AI CoE Functions

``` text
AI Center of Excellence
├── Strategy
├── Governance
├── Architecture
├── Standards
├── Training
├── Consulting
├── Innovation
├── Asset Reuse
└── Performance Management
```

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Core Roles

  Role                 Responsibility
  -------------------- -------------------------
  AI CoE Lead          Strategic leadership
  AI Architect         Enterprise architecture
  Governance Manager   Policies and compliance
  AI Engineer          Platform enablement
  Data Scientist       Model development
  Change Manager       Adoption and enablement

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Decision Rules

  Condition                    Action
  ---------------------------- ---------------------------
  New AI initiative            Review through AI CoE
  Enterprise standard needed   Publish standard
  Capability gap identified    Launch enablement program
  Reusable asset created       Register in Asset Catalog

# Best Practices

-   Balance governance with innovation.
-   Encourage knowledge sharing.
-   Measure business outcomes, not only technical metrics.
-   Continuously update standards.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Common Mistakes

-   Over-centralizing all AI work.
-   Lack of executive sponsorship.
-   Ignoring business collaboration.
-   Failing to measure value creation.
