# PB-089 --- Digital Asset Management

> **Module:** 11-enterprise-assets **Playbook ID:** PB-089 **Version:**
> 1.0.0 **Status:** Stable

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Summary

Establish an Enterprise Digital Asset Management (DAM) framework to
centrally manage digital assets throughout their lifecycle, ensuring
discoverability, security, governance, reuse, and business value.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

Provide a standardized approach for creating, organizing, storing,
classifying, versioning, securing, distributing, monitoring, archiving,
and retiring enterprise digital assets.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Value

-   Centralize digital assets.
-   Improve asset discoverability.
-   Increase reuse.
-   Protect intellectual property.
-   Reduce storage and operational costs.

# Digital Asset Lifecycle

1.  Create or acquire asset.
2.  Classify and tag metadata.
3.  Store in repository.
4.  Review and approve.
5.  Publish and distribute.
6.  Monitor usage.
7.  Archive inactive assets.
8.  Retire obsolete assets.

# Decision Rules

  Condition                 Action
  ------------------------- -------------------
  New asset created         Register and tag
  Duplicate asset           Consolidate
  Copyright issue           Restrict access
  End of retention period   Archive or retire

-   Apply role-based access control.
-   Track asset usage continuously.
-   Archive obsolete assets regularly.
