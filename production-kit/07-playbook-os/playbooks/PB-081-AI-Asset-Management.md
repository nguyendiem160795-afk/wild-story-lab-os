# PB-081 --- AI Asset Management

> **Module:** 11-enterprise-assets **Playbook ID:** PB-081 **Version:**
> 1.0.0 **Status:** Stable

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Summary

Establish an Enterprise AI Asset Management framework for identifying,
governing, tracking, optimizing, and retiring AI assets throughout their
lifecycle.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

Provide a standardized approach to manage AI assets including models,
prompts, datasets, APIs, agents, workflows, infrastructure, licenses,
and intellectual property.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Value

-   Improve asset visibility.
-   Reduce duplicate investments.
-   Increase asset reuse.
-   Strengthen governance.
-   Lower lifecycle costs.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

-   PB-080 Business Continuity completed.

# Asset Lifecycle

1.  Identify assets.
2.  Classify and register assets.
3.  Assign ownership.
4.  Monitor usage and value.
5.  Maintain and update assets.
6.  Optimize utilization.
7.  Audit compliance.
8.  Retire obsolete assets.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Decision Rules

  Condition               Action
  ----------------------- ----------------------------
  New asset created       Register in Asset Register
  Duplicate asset found   Consolidate or retire
  Low utilization         Review business value
  End-of-life reached     Execute retirement plan

-   Review asset utilization regularly.
-   Assign accountable owners.
