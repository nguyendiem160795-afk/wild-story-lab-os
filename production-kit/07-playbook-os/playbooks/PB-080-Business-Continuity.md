# PB-080 --- Business Continuity

> **Module:** 10-enterprise-operations **Playbook ID:** PB-080
> **Version:** 1.0.0 **Status:** Stable

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Summary

Establish a Business Continuity Management (BCM) framework that enables
the enterprise to continue delivering critical AI-enabled business
services during and after disruptive events.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

Provide a standardized approach for preparing, responding, recovering,
and continuously improving organizational resilience across people,
processes, technology, facilities, suppliers, and AI services.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Value

-   Maintain critical business operations.
-   Minimize financial and operational impact.
-   Increase organizational resilience.
-   Improve stakeholder confidence.
-   Support regulatory and contractual obligations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

-   PB-079 Disaster Recovery completed.

# BCM Lifecycle

1.  Identify critical business processes.
2.  Perform Business Impact Analysis.
3.  Assess continuity risks.
4.  Develop continuity strategies.
5.  Prepare response and recovery plans.
6.  Test continuity capabilities.
7.  Activate BCP during disruptions.
8.  Review lessons learned and improve BCM.

# Decision Rules

  Condition                          Action
  ---------------------------------- ------------------------------------
  Major business disruption          Activate Business Continuity Plan
  Critical supplier unavailable      Invoke alternate supplier strategy
  Recovery objectives not achieved   Escalate executive response
  Exercise completed                 Update BCM documentation

# Best Practices

-   Test continuity plans regularly.
-   Keep stakeholder communication clear.
-   Coordinate BCM with Disaster Recovery.
-   Review business priorities annually.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Common Mistakes

-   Assuming DR alone ensures continuity.
-   Outdated contact lists.
-   Untested continuity plans.
-   Ignoring third-party dependencies.
