# PB-071 --- AI Service Management

> **Module:** 10-enterprise-operations **Playbook ID:** PB-071
> **Version:** 1.0.0 **Status:** Stable

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Summary

Establish an Enterprise AI Service Management framework to design,
deliver, operate, support, and continuously improve AI services
throughout their lifecycle.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

Provide standardized service management practices that ensure AI
services are reliable, secure, measurable, and aligned with business
objectives.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Value

-   Improve AI service reliability.
-   Increase customer satisfaction.
-   Standardize AI service operations.
-   Reduce operational disruptions.
-   Support scalable AI service delivery.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Prerequisites

-   PB-070 Operational Excellence completed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Service Lifecycle

1.  Design AI services.
2.  Publish services to the catalog.
3.  Deploy and operate services.
4.  Monitor service health.
5.  Manage incidents and requests.
6.  Review SLA performance.
7.  Improve service quality.
8.  Retire obsolete services.

-   Monitor user experience continuously.
-   Automate repetitive support tasks.
-   Review SLAs regularly.

-   Undefined service ownership.
-   Poor monitoring coverage.
