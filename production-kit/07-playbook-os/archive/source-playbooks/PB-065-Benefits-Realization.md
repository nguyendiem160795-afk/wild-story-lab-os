# PB-065 --- Benefits Realization

> **Module:** 09-enterprise-execution **Playbook ID:** PB-065
> **Version:** 1.0.0 **Status:** Stable

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Summary

Establish a Benefits Realization framework to identify, quantify, track,
and optimize the business value generated by AI portfolios, programs,
projects, and products throughout their lifecycle.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

Ensure every AI investment delivers measurable strategic, financial,
operational, and customer value through structured planning, governance,
and continuous measurement.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Value

-   Maximize return on AI investments.
-   Align outcomes with strategic objectives.
-   Improve executive decision-making.
-   Increase accountability for value delivery.
-   Enable continuous optimization.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Prerequisites

-   PB-064 AI Product Management completed.

# Benefits Realization Lifecycle

1.  Identify expected benefits.
2.  Define measurable KPIs.
3.  Assign benefit owners.
4.  Establish baseline values.
5.  Monitor realized benefits.
6.  Analyze variances.
7.  Optimize improvement initiatives.
8.  Capture lessons learned.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Benefits

# Decision Rules

  Condition                     Action
  ----------------------------- ---------------------------
  Benefit below target          Initiate improvement plan
  KPI exceeded                  Document best practice
  Benefit no longer relevant    Update benefit register
  Strategic priorities change   Reassess expected value

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Best Practices

-   Measure outcomes, not outputs.
-   Review benefits regularly.
-   Link every benefit to a business objective.
-   Continue measurement after deployment.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Common Mistakes

-   No benefit ownership.
-   Undefined baselines.
-   Measuring only technical performance.
-   Stopping measurement after project completion.
