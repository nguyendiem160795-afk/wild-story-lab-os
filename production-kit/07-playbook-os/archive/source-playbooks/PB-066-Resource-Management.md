# PB-066 --- Resource Management

> **Module:** 09-enterprise-execution **Playbook ID:** PB-066
> **Version:** 1.0.0 **Status:** Stable

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Summary

Define a standardized Resource Management framework to plan, allocate,
optimize, monitor, and continuously improve the utilization of people,
budget, technology, infrastructure, data, and AI capabilities across
enterprise AI initiatives.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

Ensure the right resources are available at the right time to maximize
delivery performance, business value, and operational efficiency.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Value

-   Optimize resource utilization.
-   Improve delivery predictability.
-   Reduce bottlenecks.
-   Balance capacity with demand.
-   Increase return on AI investments.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Prerequisites

-   PB-065 Benefits Realization completed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Resource Management Lifecycle

1.  Identify required resources.
2.  Assess available capacity.
3.  Allocate resources based on priorities.
4.  Monitor utilization and performance.
5.  Resolve resource conflicts.
6.  Optimize allocation continuously.
7.  Forecast future demand.
8.  Capture lessons learned.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

``` text
Enterprise Resources
├── People

├── AI Platforms
├── Data Assets
├── Software Licenses
└── External Vendors
```

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Decision Rules

  Condition                    Action
  ---------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Resource shortage            Escalate and rebalance allocation
  Underutilization detected    Reassign resources
  Budget exceeded              Review funding and scope
  Strategic priority changes   Reallocate resources

# Best Practices

-   Maintain a centralized resource registry.
-   Review allocations regularly.
-   Balance specialist and shared resources.
-   Forecast demand proactively.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Common Mistakes

-   Overallocation of key personnel.
-   Ignoring future capacity needs.
-   Poor visibility into utilization.
-   Delayed resource reallocation.
