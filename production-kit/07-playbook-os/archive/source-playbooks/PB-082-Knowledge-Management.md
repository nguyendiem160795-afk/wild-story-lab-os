# PB-082 --- Knowledge Management

> **Module:** 11-enterprise-assets **Playbook ID:** PB-082 **Version:**
> 1.0.0 **Status:** Stable

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Summary

Establish an Enterprise Knowledge Management framework to capture,
organize, govern, share, reuse, and continuously improve organizational
knowledge across the AI ecosystem.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

Provide a standardized approach for managing enterprise knowledge
including playbooks, SOPs, runbooks, lessons learned, prompts, model
cards, architecture, and technical documentation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Value

-   Preserve organizational knowledge.
-   Accelerate onboarding.
-   Improve decision quality.
-   Increase knowledge reuse.
-   Reduce repeated mistakes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Prerequisites

-   PB-081 AI Asset Management completed.

# Knowledge Lifecycle

1.  Capture knowledge.
2.  Classify and tag content.
3.  Validate and approve.
4.  Publish to the knowledge base.
5.  Share and promote reuse.
6.  Maintain and update.
7.  Archive obsolete knowledge.
8.  Measure knowledge effectiveness.

# Decision Rules

  Condition               Action
  ----------------------- ----------------------
  New knowledge created   Capture and classify
  Content outdated        Review and update
  Duplicate knowledge     Consolidate
  Obsolete knowledge      Archive
