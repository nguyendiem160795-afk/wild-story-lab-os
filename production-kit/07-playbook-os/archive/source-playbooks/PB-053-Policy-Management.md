# PB-053 --- Policy Management

> **Module:** 08-enterprise-governance **Playbook ID:** PB-053
> **Version:** 1.0.0 **Status:** Stable

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Summary

Establish a standardized policy management framework for creating,
reviewing, approving, publishing, maintaining, and retiring enterprise
policies governing AI systems and the Playbook OS.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

Ensure policies remain consistent, traceable, version-controlled, and
enforceable across all workflows, AI agents, and organizational
processes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Value

-   Standardize governance policies.
-   Improve regulatory compliance.
-   Reduce policy ambiguity.
-   Enable organization-wide policy enforcement.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Prerequisites

-   PB-052 Enterprise Knowledge Graph completed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Outputs

-   Policy Catalog
-   Approved Policy Documents
-   Policy Version History
-   Policy Compliance Report

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Workflow

1.  Identify policy requirements.
2.  Draft policy.
3.  Review with stakeholders.
4.  Obtain approvals.
5.  Publish policy.
6.  Communicate policy changes.
7.  Monitor compliance.
8.  Review and update periodically.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Policy Lifecycle

``` text
Policy Lifecycle
├── Draft
├── Review
├── Approved
├── Published
├── Active
├── Revision
├── Deprecated
└── Archived
```

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Decision Rules

  Condition                    Action
  ---------------------------- --------------------------------
  New regulatory requirement   Draft or update policy
  Policy approved              Publish organization-wide
  Compliance issue             Initiate corrective action
  Policy obsolete              Archive with history preserved

# Best Practices

-   Use standardized policy templates.
-   Keep language clear and enforceable.
-   Review policies on a scheduled cadence.
-   Link policies to related Playbooks and controls.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Common Mistakes

-   Uncontrolled policy changes.
-   Missing approval records.
-   Poor version management.
-   Lack of communication after policy updates.
