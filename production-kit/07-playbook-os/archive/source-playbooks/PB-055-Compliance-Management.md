# PB-055 --- Compliance Management

> **Module:** 08-enterprise-governance **Playbook ID:** PB-055
> **Version:** 1.0.0 **Status:** Stable

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Summary

Establish a standardized compliance management framework to ensure AI
systems, Playbooks, workflows, agents, and operational processes
consistently comply with internal policies, contractual obligations, and
applicable regulations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

Provide a repeatable process for identifying compliance requirements,
assessing adherence, managing corrective actions, and continuously
improving compliance across the Playbook OS.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Value

-   Strengthen regulatory readiness.
-   Reduce compliance risk.
-   Improve governance transparency.
-   Simplify audits.
-   Increase stakeholder confidence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Prerequisites

-   PB-054 AI Risk Management completed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Inputs

## Optional

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Outputs

-   Compliance Register
-   Compliance Assessment Report
-   Corrective Action Plan
-   Compliance Dashboard

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Workflow

1.  Identify applicable compliance obligations.
2.  Map requirements to Playbooks and workflows.
3.  Assess current compliance status.
4.  Identify gaps and non-conformities.
5.  Define corrective actions.
6.  Track remediation progress.
7.  Verify compliance effectiveness.
8.  Continuously monitor compliance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Decision Rules

  Condition                   Action
  --------------------------- ----------------------------
  New regulation issued       Perform impact assessment
  Non-compliance detected     Launch corrective action
  Critical compliance issue   Escalate immediately
  Requirement retired         Archive with audit history

# Best Practices

-   Review requirements regularly.
-   Maintain traceability from policy to execution.
-   Automate evidence collection where possible.
-   Integrate compliance into daily workflows.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Common Mistakes

-   Treating compliance as a one-time activity.
-   Missing ownership for corrective actions.
-   Poor evidence management.
-   Delayed regulatory updates.
