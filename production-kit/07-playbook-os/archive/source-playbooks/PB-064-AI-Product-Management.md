# PB-064 --- AI Product Management

> **Module:** 09-enterprise-execution **Playbook ID:** PB-064
> **Version:** 1.0.0 **Status:** Stable

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Summary

Establish a standardized AI Product Management framework that governs
the complete lifecycle of AI products, from opportunity discovery
through retirement, ensuring continuous value delivery and alignment
with enterprise strategy.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

Provide a repeatable approach for defining vision, prioritizing
features, managing roadmaps, measuring outcomes, and continuously
improving AI products.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Value

-   Deliver customer-centric AI products.
-   Improve product-market fit.
-   Prioritize high-value features.
-   Increase adoption and customer satisfaction.
-   Maximize long-term business value.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Product Lifecycle

1.  Discover customer problems.
2.  Define product vision and strategy.
3.  Prioritize roadmap and backlog.
4.  Build and validate AI capabilities.
5.  Release product increments.
6.  Measure adoption and business outcomes.
7.  Gather feedback and iterate.
8.  Retire or evolve the product.

# Decision Rules

  Condition                     Action
  ----------------------------- -------------------------------
  New customer opportunity      Evaluate business value
  Feature request               Prioritize in backlog
  KPI below target              Launch improvement initiative
  Product reaches end-of-life   Execute retirement plan

# Best Practices

-   Focus on customer outcomes.
-   Validate assumptions with data.
-   Prioritize continuously.
-   Measure value after every release.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Common Mistakes

-   Building features without validation.
-   Ignoring user feedback.
-   Measuring outputs instead of outcomes.
-   Allowing roadmap priorities to stagnate.
