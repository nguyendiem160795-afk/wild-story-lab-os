# PB-047 --- Content Portfolio Management

> **Module:** 07-playbook-os (Advanced) **Playbook ID:** PB-047
> **Version:** 1.0.0 **Status:** Stable

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Summary

Establish a structured approach to managing the organization's complete
content portfolio, ensuring every content asset is categorized, tracked,
evaluated, and optimized throughout its lifecycle.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

Provide governance for planning, organizing, prioritizing, maintaining,
and retiring content assets across all channels and business objectives.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Value

-   Improve content visibility and governance.
-   Maximize reuse of high-performing assets.
-   Reduce duplicate content creation.
-   Align content investments with business goals.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Prerequisites

-   PB-046 Multi-Platform Distribution Strategy completed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Inputs

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Outputs

-   Content Portfolio Catalog
-   Content Classification Matrix
-   Portfolio Health Report
-   Content Roadmap

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Workflow

1.  Inventory all content assets.
2.  Classify content by type, topic, audience, and lifecycle.
3.  Evaluate portfolio performance.
4.  Identify gaps, duplication, and opportunities.
5.  Prioritize content initiatives.
6.  Update portfolio roadmap.
7.  Archive obsolete content.
8.  Publish portfolio status report.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Portfolio Structure

``` text
Content Portfolio
├── Educational
├── Entertainment
├── Marketing
├── Evergreen
├── Seasonal
├── Experimental
├── Archived
└── Retired
```

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Decision Rules

  Condition                  Action
  -------------------------- -------------------
  High-performing content    Expand and reuse
  Low-performing content     Refresh or retire
  Duplicate content          Consolidate
  Strategic gap identified   Plan new content

# Best Practices

-   Review the portfolio regularly.
-   Use consistent taxonomy.
-   Prioritize evergreen assets.
-   Monitor lifecycle transitions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Common Mistakes

-   Ignoring outdated content.
-   Poor metadata quality.
-   Duplicate asset creation.
-   Missing strategic alignment.
