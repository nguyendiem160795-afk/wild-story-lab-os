# PB-090 --- Enterprise Asset Optimization

> **Module:** 11-enterprise-assets **Playbook ID:** PB-090 **Version:**
> 1.0.0 **Status:** Stable

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Summary

Establish an Enterprise Asset Optimization framework to continuously
evaluate, optimize, consolidate, and maximize the value of enterprise
assets across the AI ecosystem.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

Provide a standardized methodology for measuring utilization, cost,
quality, business value, and lifecycle maturity of enterprise assets,
enabling continuous optimization and strategic investment.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Value

-   Maximize return on enterprise assets.
-   Reduce redundant assets and operational costs.
-   Improve asset utilization.
-   Increase reuse across business units.
-   Strengthen governance and decision-making.

# Optimization Lifecycle

1.  Inventory enterprise assets.
2.  Measure utilization and cost.
3.  Evaluate quality and business value.
4.  Identify duplication and gaps.
5.  Optimize or consolidate assets.
6.  Retire low-value assets.
7.  Monitor optimization outcomes.
8.  Continuously improve the portfolio.

# Decision Rules

  Condition               Action
  ----------------------- -------------------------
  Low utilization         Optimize or retire
  Duplicate assets        Consolidate
  High business value     Prioritize investment
  High maintenance cost   Review ROI and optimize

# Best Practices

-   Measure before optimizing.
-   Use objective KPIs.
-   Review the portfolio regularly.
-   Balance cost, risk, and business value.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Common Mistakes

-   Optimizing without metrics.
-   Ignoring duplicate assets.
-   Focusing only on cost.
-   No continuous review cycle.
