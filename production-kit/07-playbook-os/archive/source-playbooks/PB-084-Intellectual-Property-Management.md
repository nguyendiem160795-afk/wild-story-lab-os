# PB-084 --- Intellectual Property Management

> **Module:** 11-enterprise-assets **Playbook ID:** PB-084 **Version:**
> 1.0.0 **Status:** Stable

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Summary

Establish an Enterprise Intellectual Property (IP) Management framework
to identify, protect, govern, commercialize, and retire intellectual
property generated throughout the AI lifecycle.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

Provide a standardized approach for managing AI-related intellectual
property including AI models, prompts, datasets, algorithms, source
code, trademarks, patents, copyrights, trade secrets, and licensing
agreements.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Value

-   Protect enterprise innovation.
-   Maximize IP value and commercialization.
-   Reduce legal and compliance risks.
-   Strengthen competitive advantage.
-   Improve governance of digital assets.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Intellectual Property Lifecycle

1.  Identify intellectual property.
2.  Classify ownership and rights.
3.  Register and document IP assets.
4.  Protect through legal and technical controls.
5.  Monitor usage and compliance.
6.  Commercialize where appropriate.
7.  Review and audit IP portfolio.
8.  Retire or transfer IP assets.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Decision Rules

  Condition                  Action
  -------------------------- ---------------------------
  New IP created             Register and classify
  Ownership unclear          Escalate for legal review
  License expires            Renew or retire
  IP infringement detected   Initiate legal response

# Best Practices

-   Protect IP as early as possible.
-   Maintain clear ownership records.
-   Review licensing regularly.
-   Integrate IP governance into AI development.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Common Mistakes

-   Unregistered intellectual property.
-   Undefined ownership.
-   Expired licenses.
-   Poor documentation of proprietary assets.
