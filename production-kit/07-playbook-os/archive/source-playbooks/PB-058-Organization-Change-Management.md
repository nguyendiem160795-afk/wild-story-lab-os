# PB-058 --- Organization Change Management

> **Module:** 08-enterprise-governance **Playbook ID:** PB-058
> **Version:** 1.0.0 **Status:** Stable

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Summary

Provide a structured framework for planning, communicating,
implementing, measuring, and sustaining organizational change associated
with AI adoption and Playbook OS transformation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

Ensure people, processes, and culture successfully adapt to AI-enabled
operating models while minimizing disruption and maximizing adoption.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Value

-   Accelerate AI adoption.
-   Reduce resistance to change.
-   Improve employee readiness.
-   Increase long-term transformation success.
-   Align organizational objectives.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Prerequisites

-   PB-057 AI Asset Management completed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Inputs

## Required

-   Transformation Strategy
-   Stakeholder Register
-   AI Governance Framework
-   Training Plans
-   Communication Plan

## Optional

-   Employee Surveys
-   Adoption Metrics
-   Lessons Learned

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Outputs

-   Change Management Plan
-   Stakeholder Engagement Plan
-   Adoption Dashboard
-   Readiness Assessment
-   Post-Implementation Review

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Workflow

1.  Assess organizational readiness.
2.  Identify stakeholders and impacts.
3.  Develop communication strategy.
4.  Execute training and enablement.
5.  Monitor adoption and feedback.
6.  Address resistance and risks.
7.  Measure business outcomes.
8.  Reinforce and sustain change.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Change Management Framework

``` text
Organization Change
├── Vision
├── Leadership
├── Stakeholders
├── Communication
├── Training
├── Adoption
├── Reinforcement
└── Continuous Improvement
```

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Decision Rules

  Condition               Action
  ----------------------- --------------------------------------
  Low readiness           Expand training and communication
  High resistance         Escalate change interventions
  Adoption below target   Execute recovery plan
  Objectives achieved     Transition to continuous improvement

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Best Practices

-   Communicate early and often.
-   Engage leadership visibly.
-   Measure adoption continuously.
-   Celebrate milestones and successes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Common Mistakes

-   Focusing only on technology.
-   Underestimating resistance.
-   Insufficient communication.
-   Lack of executive sponsorship.
