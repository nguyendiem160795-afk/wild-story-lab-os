# Executive Summary

Retire an AI asset in a controlled manner after it reaches the end of
its operational lifecycle while preserving governance records,
compliance evidence, and historical traceability.

# Purpose

Safely decommission AI assets that are obsolete, replaced, no longer
supported, or no longer deliver business value.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Value

-   Reduce operational risk
-   Eliminate obsolete assets
-   Lower maintenance costs
-   Improve governance
-   Complete the asset lifecycle

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Prerequisites

-   PB-091 through PB-099 completed
-   Retirement request approved
-   Compliance review completed
-   Retention requirements verified

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Inputs

-   Asset ID
-   Retirement request
-   Compliance records
-   Archive status
-   Business approval

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Outputs

-   Retirement confirmation
-   Updated asset registry
-   Lifecycle closure record
-   Final audit log
-   Retirement report

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Workflow

1.  Confirm retirement approval.
2.  Verify legal and retention requirements.
3.  Ensure all required assets are archived.
4.  Notify affected stakeholders.
5.  Change asset status to Retired.
6.  Update the enterprise asset registry.
7.  Preserve historical records.
8.  Record the final audit entry.
9.  Publish the retirement report.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Decision Rules

  Condition                      Action
  ------------------------------ ---------------------
  Active dependency detected     Delay retirement
  Compliance review incomplete   Reject request
  All requirements satisfied     Complete retirement

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Success Criteria

-   Asset is permanently retired.
-   Governance records remain complete.
-   Lifecycle is formally closed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Deliverables

-   Retirement Report
-   Registry Update
-   Lifecycle Closure Record
-   Final Audit Record

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Best Practices

-   Verify downstream dependencies before retirement.
-   Preserve historical evidence.
-   Communicate retirement to all stakeholders.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Common Mistakes

-   Retiring assets still in use.
-   Removing audit history.
-   Ignoring retention obligations.
