# PB-060 --- Enterprise AI Operating Model

> **Module:** 08-enterprise-governance **Playbook ID:** PB-060
> **Version:** 1.0.0 **Status:** Stable

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Summary

Define the Enterprise AI Operating Model that aligns strategy,
governance, people, processes, technology, and performance management
into a unified framework for delivering, operating, and continuously
improving AI capabilities at enterprise scale.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

Provide a standardized operating model for planning, building,
deploying, governing, measuring, and evolving AI initiatives across the
organization.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Value

-   Align AI with business strategy.
-   Standardize enterprise AI operations.
-   Improve delivery efficiency.
-   Increase governance maturity.
-   Enable scalable AI transformation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Prerequisites

-   PB-059 AI Center of Excellence completed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Workflow

1.  Align AI strategy with business objectives.
2.  Define governance, roles, and decision rights.
3.  Standardize AI delivery processes.
4.  Establish enterprise operating procedures.
5.  Monitor KPIs and operational health.
6.  Manage risks and compliance.
7.  Capture lessons learned.
8.  Continuously optimize the operating model.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Enterprise Roles

  Role                  Responsibility
  --------------------- --------------------------
  Executive Sponsor     Strategic direction
  AI CoE                Standards and enablement
  Business Owner        Value realization
  Product Owner         AI product lifecycle
  AI Engineering Team   Delivery and operations
  Governance Board      Oversight and approvals

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Decision Rules

  Condition                 Action
  ------------------------- -------------------------------------
  New AI initiative         Apply operating model lifecycle
  Governance exception      Escalate to Governance Board
  KPI below target          Launch improvement plan
  Operating model updated   Publish new version and communicate

# Best Practices

-   Align technology with business outcomes.
-   Keep governance lightweight but effective.
-   Review KPIs regularly.
-   Evolve the operating model based on feedback.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Common Mistakes

-   Operating without standardized governance.
-   Undefined ownership.
-   Measuring only technical metrics.
-   Ignoring organizational adoption.
