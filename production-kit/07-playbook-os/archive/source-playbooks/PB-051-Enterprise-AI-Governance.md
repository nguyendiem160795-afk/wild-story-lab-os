# PB-051 --- Enterprise AI Governance

> **Module:** 08-enterprise-governance **Playbook ID:** PB-051
> **Version:** 1.0.0 **Status:** Stable

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Summary

Define an enterprise-wide governance framework for AI systems, ensuring
that policies, roles, decision rights, compliance, risk management, and
operational controls are consistently applied across the Playbook OS.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

Provide a governance model that enables AI adoption at scale while
maintaining accountability, transparency, and regulatory compliance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Value

-   Standardize AI governance.
-   Reduce legal and operational risk.
-   Clarify ownership and decision authority.
-   Improve audit readiness.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Prerequisites

-   PB-050 Self-Improving Playbook System completed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Inputs

## Required

-   Organizational Policies
-   AI Governance Charter
-   Risk Register
-   Compliance Requirements

## Optional

-   External Audit Reports
-   Regulatory Updates
-   Executive Directives

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Outputs

-   AI Governance Framework
-   RACI Matrix
-   Governance Dashboard
-   Compliance Audit Report

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Workflow

1.  Define governance objectives.
2.  Assign ownership and accountability.
3.  Establish policies and standards.
4.  Classify AI systems by risk.
5.  Monitor compliance.
6.  Review governance metrics.
7.  Resolve exceptions.
8.  Continuously improve governance.
