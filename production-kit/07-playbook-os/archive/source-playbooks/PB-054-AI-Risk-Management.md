# PB-054 --- AI Risk Management

> **Module:** 08-enterprise-governance **Playbook ID:** PB-054
> **Version:** 1.0.0 **Status:** Stable

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Summary

Establish a comprehensive AI risk management framework to identify,
assess, mitigate, monitor, and continuously improve the management of
risks associated with AI systems, workflows, models, and agents.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

Provide a structured and repeatable process for managing AI-related
risks while balancing innovation, operational efficiency, and regulatory
compliance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Value

-   Reduce AI operational risks.
-   Improve resilience and business continuity.
-   Strengthen governance and trust.
-   Support regulatory compliance.
-   Enable risk-informed AI adoption.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Prerequisites

-   PB-053 Policy Management completed.
-   Enterprise AI Governance Framework established.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Inputs

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Outputs

-   AI Risk Register
-   Risk Assessment Report
-   Risk Treatment Plan
-   Residual Risk Report
-   Risk Dashboard

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Workflow

1.  Identify AI risks.
2.  Classify risks by category and severity.
3.  Assess likelihood and business impact.
4.  Define mitigation strategies.
5.  Assign risk owners.
6.  Monitor key risk indicators (KRIs).
7.  Review residual risks.
8.  Continuously improve the risk framework.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Risk Treatment Options

  Strategy   Description
  ---------- -------------------------------------------
  Avoid      Eliminate the risk source
  Reduce     Apply mitigation controls
  Transfer   Share risk through contracts or insurance
  Accept     Accept residual risk with approval

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Decision Rules

  Condition                  Action
  -------------------------- -----------------------------
  Critical risk identified   Immediate escalation
  High residual risk         Executive approval required
  Risk threshold exceeded    Trigger mitigation workflow
  Risk retired               Archive with audit history

------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Best Practices

-   Maintain a living risk register.
-   Review risks after major AI changes.
-   Use quantitative and qualitative assessments.
-   Integrate risk monitoring into daily operations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Common Mistakes

-   Assessing risks only once.
-   Ignoring third-party AI risks.
-   Failing to assign ownership.
-   Treating compliance as the only risk dimension.
