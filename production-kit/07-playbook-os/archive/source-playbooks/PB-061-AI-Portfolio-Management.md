# PB-061 --- AI Portfolio Management

> **Module:** 09-enterprise-execution **Playbook ID:** PB-061
> **Version:** 1.0.0 **Status:** Stable

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Summary

Define a governance framework for selecting, prioritizing, funding,
monitoring, and optimizing an enterprise portfolio of AI initiatives to
maximize strategic value and resource utilization.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

Provide a standardized approach for managing multiple AI programs and
projects as a single enterprise portfolio aligned with business
strategy.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Value

-   Align investments with strategic objectives.
-   Improve prioritization and resource allocation.
-   Increase portfolio visibility.
-   Balance risk, cost, and value.
-   Improve AI ROI.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Prerequisites

-   PB-060 Enterprise AI Operating Model completed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Portfolio Lifecycle

1.  Capture AI initiatives.
2.  Evaluate strategic alignment.
3.  Assess value, cost, risk, and feasibility.
4.  Prioritize initiatives.
5.  Allocate funding and resources.
6.  Monitor execution and KPIs.
7.  Review portfolio performance.
8.  Rebalance the portfolio continuously.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Portfolio Categories

``` text
AI Portfolio
├── Strategic Initiatives
├── Operational Improvements
├── Innovation Projects
├── Compliance Programs
├── Infrastructure
├── Data Platforms
└── Research & Experimentation
```

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Decision Rules

  Condition              Action
  ---------------------- --------------------------------
  High strategic value   Prioritize for funding
  Resource constraint    Rebalance portfolio
  Low ROI                Review or terminate initiative
  New opportunity        Reassess portfolio priorities

# Best Practices

-   Review priorities quarterly.
-   Balance innovation and operational work.
-   Measure business outcomes.
-   Retire low-value initiatives.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Common Mistakes

-   Funding every idea.
-   Ignoring resource capacity.
-   Measuring only technical success.
-   Failing to rebalance the portfolio.
